07 / ABOUT

TRIAD is not a magazine.

It is a platform for creative expression, independent thinking and cultural exchange.

Fashion is the starting point. Art, music, photography and design are where it goes.

We publish what we believe in. We archive what deserves to last. We ignore the rest.

I

Form

Fashion. Cut, cloth, construction. The body as the first site of culture.

II

Signal

Music and sound. The noise that moves through a scene before anyone names it.

III

Image

Art, photography, design. What remains when the moment is gone.

THE SYSTEM

Symbols.

A visual language built for print, garments, covers and screens.

LIBRARY

Triangle. Triad. Pyramid.

Ten signs from geometry, alchemy, music and architecture — redrawn for TRIAD. Free to use on garments, covers and screens.

  • S/01 — Borromäische Ringe Borromean Rings

    Three rings held together — remove one and the other two fall apart. No force stands alone.

    SVG ↓
  • S/02 — Feuer Fire △

    Alchemical fire. Upward energy. Used for IMAGE.

    SVG ↓
  • S/03 — Luft Air

    Alchemical air. Breath, sound, signal. Used for SIGNAL.

    SVG ↓
  • S/04 — Erde Earth

    Alchemical earth. Matter, cloth, the body. Used for FORM.

    SVG ↓
  • S/05 — Wasser Water ▽

    Alchemical water. The inverted triangle — reserved for night editions.

    SVG ↓
  • S/06 — Sierpiński-Dreieck Sierpiński Triangle

    Triangles inside triangles, endlessly. The archive: every piece contains the whole.

    SVG ↓
  • S/07 — Tonnetz Tonnetz

    In the tone lattice every triangle is a triad. C–E–G, lit lime: the Dreiklang.

    SVG ↓
  • S/08 — Dreiklang Dreiklang

    Root, third, fifth. Three notes stacked in thirds — the smallest harmony.

    SVG ↓
  • S/09 — Pyramide Pyramid

    Three tiers, seen from the side and from above. Structure built to outlast its makers.

    SVG ↓
  • S/10 — Delta Delta + + +

    Δ means change. Three plus marks: three forces added together.

    SVG ↓
