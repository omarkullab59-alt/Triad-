Mara Solenne builds looks from flea-market archives and garments nobody else wanted. Her Paris apartment is stacked floor to ceiling with decades of clothing, each rail a different era.

She calls her method designing for the third person in the room: the absent maker whose work survives only as an object. Repairs, patches and damage are what she looks for first.

## In her words

> Anything that looks like it is trying to be photographed, I leave at the market.
