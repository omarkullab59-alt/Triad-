Hanne Wolter opened a basement shop in Kreuzberg the year the Wall came down. No sign, no website — just a door painted the colour of whatever paint was left that month.

Three generations of stylists, musicians and art students have dressed from her rails. She still prices everything by hand on brown paper tags.

## Why she matters

Before vintage was a category, it was Hanne's basement. Ask anyone in the scene where they found their first real coat.
