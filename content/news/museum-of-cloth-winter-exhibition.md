A fictional Museum of Cloth has opened a winter exhibition dedicated to workwear, covering uniforms, overalls and aprons from the last two centuries.

The curators present the garments without mannequins, laid flat in long vitrines, so that repairs and wear patterns become the focus.

A programme of talks with designers who reference workwear accompanies the exhibition.
