Fictional streetwear label Vanta Works has released an outerwear line with no visible branding. The only identifier is a six-digit code printed inside the collar.

The release continues a move across independent streetwear toward quieter signals, which TRIAD explored in the feature After the Logo.

The pieces were released online without prior announcement.
