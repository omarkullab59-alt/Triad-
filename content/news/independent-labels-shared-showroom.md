Twelve fictional independent labels have formed a cooperative to share one showroom during the selling season, splitting rent and staff costs.

Buyers book one appointment and see all twelve collections. Each label keeps full control over pricing and production.

Members describe the model as a practical answer to rising costs for small brands.
