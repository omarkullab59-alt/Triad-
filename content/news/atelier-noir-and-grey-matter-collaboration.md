Two fictional independent labels, Atelier Noir and Grey Matter, have announced a joint knitwear capsule produced in one family-run mill.

The capsule consists of six pieces in undyed wool. Each label designed three; neither saw the other's designs until the samples arrived.

The release is planned in very small quantities and sold only at a single pop-up.
