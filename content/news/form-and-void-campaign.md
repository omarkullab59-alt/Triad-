The fictional label Form & Void has released a campaign in which no person appears. Garments are shown draped over chairs, hung on door handles and folded on stairs.

The label says the images are meant to show clothes as they actually live most of the time — waiting.

The campaign was photographed in a single apartment over one afternoon.
