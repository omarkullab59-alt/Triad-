The fictional label Halden Studio presented its first collection on the lowest level of a multi-storey car park. Guests stood between parked cars; the models walked the ramp.

The collection focused on outerwear cut from reclaimed tarpaulin and wool felt. Lighting came from the headlights of a row of borrowed cars.

Halden Studio describes the location as a comment on how fashion week distributes space: the official venues upstairs, everyone else below.
