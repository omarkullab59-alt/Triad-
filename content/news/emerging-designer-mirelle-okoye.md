Fictional graduate Mirelle Okoye has been named the winner of a design school's annual graduate award for a collection of hand-painted tailoring.

Each jacket was fully constructed before being painted, so the brushstrokes follow seams and darts rather than flat pattern pieces.

Okoye says she plans to continue producing to order rather than seasonally.
